The total world population of reticulated giraffes is thought to be roughly 16,000, half the number estimated in the 1980s, and of those only about 8,500 are found in the wild. Named after their striking coats, these animals live wild in parts of Kenya, Somalia, and Ethiopia. Our duo was photographed in Buffalo Springs National Reserve, a vital nature park in central Kenya. Reticulated giraffes live here alongside other iconic species like zebras, bush elephants, lions, hippos, and even a large number of Somali ostriches. Kenya is also home to populations of two other varieties of giraffe—the Masai and Nubian subspecies.
