The shoreline here at Gold Beach, a small city in southern Oregon, is undeniably stunning: massive sea stacks line up in the surf like ancient sentinels, while Sitka spruce trees crown the rocky hills above the shore. On a clear day you can see Humbug Mountain while strolling these sands. Venture inland from the shore and you'll find the Rogue River running through town. And it's on this river that mailboats still make their way roughly 30 miles to Agness, a community upstream from Gold Beach. This journey is one of only two mail-boat routes still operating in the United States.
