Finnish Lakeland more than earns its name. It’s been estimated that at least 55,000 lakes dot this region in eastern Finland. The entire country is reputed to have roughly 188,000 lakes, but Finnish Lakeland boasts a particularly high concentration of them. Approximately 25 per cent of the area is made up of lakes, although it seems like even more because inlets, sounds and rivers also interlace the landscape. In our image we’re taking a top-down look at a crystal clear pond and its surroundings. Finnish Lakeland is a mostly rural area, rich with coniferous forests and winding shorelines. Not surprisingly, many Finns choose to holiday here in picturesque cabins tucked under a few trees and close to the water’s edge.
