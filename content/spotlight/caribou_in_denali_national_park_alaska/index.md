One of the so-called Big Five animals of Denali National Park, the caribou is an icon of the Alaskan wilderness. This animal is a familiar inhabitant of the northern wilds of other lands far from here as well, where they're called reindeer. Caribou and reindeer are both of the Rangifer tarandus species, though there are differences between the two animals. Caribou are larger, wild, and live in northern North America and Greenland. Reindeer, on the other hand, are found in Eurasia and have been domesticated for 2,000 years. Fast fact: Caribou are the only deer species in which both males and females grow antlers.
