Canoe dock on Moraine Lake in Alberta, Canada
Banff National Park’s Moraine Lake is a blue jewel set within a remote region of the Canadian Rockies. Glacially fed and lying at over 6,000 feet in elevation, the stillness of Moraine Lake reflects the giants above it in the Valley of the Ten Peaks. It’s an exceptionally spectacular portion of landscape in an area full of jaw-dropping vistas: With more than 2,500 square miles of wilderness, Banff National Park includes a sort of greatest hits collection of Canada’s natural splendors. In our image, a stylized mix of black-and-white and color, those canoes really stand out—and look just as vibrant against the true colors of the wilderness around them.
