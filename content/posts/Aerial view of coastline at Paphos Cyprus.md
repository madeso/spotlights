Located in southwest Cyprus, an island country in the Mediterranean Sea, Paphos is the site of much historical splendour. Not surprising when you consider that human settlement here dates back to the Neolithic period. But the natural environment is just as extraordinary as the archaeological sites. We're glimpsing a bit of the incredible coastal landscape – from high above. The vivid blue of the sea meets the stark, chalk-white rock of the coastline. Many myths and legends were born from this stunning shore, including the one swirling around Aphrodite's birthplace, a sea stack just off the sands of Paphos.
