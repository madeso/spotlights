Up in the Canadian Rockies, Jasper National Park is jam-packed with breathtaking scenery, offering visitors countless opportunities to shoot photos like this one of the Sunwapta River. Not far from where this image was captured is Sunwapta Falls. The falls (a pair of upper and lower cascades) are usually at their fullest power in late spring and early summer, when the Columbia Icefield that feeds the river is in the midst of its seasonal melt. That icefield also feeds another scene of tumbling water roughly 20 miles north of here: Athabasca Falls. Both spots are accessible by car via one of the world's great scenic routes, the Icefields Parkway.
