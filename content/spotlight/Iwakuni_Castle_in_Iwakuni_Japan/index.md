One of the most renowned sightseeing attractions in Iwakuni is the ornate white castle crowning Mount Shiroyama. And while Iwakuni Castle appears centuries old, it was built in 1962. To be fair, the castle is actually a meticulous reconstruction of the original which was completed in 1608 and destroyed seven years later. That demolition wasn't due to war, but to a shogunate decree allowing only a single castle per province.

Hiking paths and a cable car lead to this grand replica castle. From its top levels, visitors get sweeping views over the town below. The panoramic scene showcases another of Iwakuni's historic treasures – the Kintai Bridge. Spanning the Nishiki River, the bridge was first built in 1673, and like the castle above it, has been carefully reconstructed to maintain its beauty and renown.
