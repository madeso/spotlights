Every spring, Longyan's cherry trees burst into color, and one of the best places to stroll among the profusion of blooms is the place we're seeing from high above in our image: Yongfu Town park. Winding through hills quilted with green tea fields and below blossoming trees are paths perfect for sightseeing. During the Qingming Festival each April, the path below will be filled with families. Qingming translates as 'Pure Brightness,' though it's also called Tomb-Sweeping Day. It's a time to honor deceased ancestors, often by cleaning and decorating their graves. The solemnity is balanced by the arrival of spring, so some Qingming observances include spending time outdoors with family, and this Longyan park is a beautiful place to gather.
