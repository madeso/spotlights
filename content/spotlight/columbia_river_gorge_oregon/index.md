Mossy Grotto Falls was discovered only relatively recently among the fern-shaded corners of Ruckle Creek. At roughly 20 feet high, it isn't remarkably tall, especially in comparison to the giants of the waterfall world, but its setting is sublime. Here, the 80-mile-long Columbia River Gorge acts as a natural border between Oregon and Washington state. Following the course of the river, the landscape goes from open grasslands in the east to lush, temperate rainforest in the west. There are approximately 90 waterfalls along the Oregon side of the Columbia Gorge alone, and tallest among them is the mighty Multnomah.
