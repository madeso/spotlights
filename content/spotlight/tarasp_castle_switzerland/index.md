For a castle to shine in this landscape of Swiss Alpine majesty, it's got to be special. Tarasp Castle is such a standout, both figuratively and literally. Begun as early as the 10th century, the hill castle was expanded through the centuries, becoming increasingly grand and impressive atop its rocky outcrop. Tarasp was abandoned in 1815, and like many a massive castle, fell into ruin. But similar to other fortresses facing that fate, this structure was saved by caring conservationists—and after more than a century of renovations, Tarasp Castle is now a Swiss heritage site of national significance.
