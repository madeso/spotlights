An ancient volcano, Piton des Neiges, gave rise to this island, a far-flung overseas department of France in the Indian Ocean. And while that volcano is dormant, another – Piton de la Fournaise – is one of the most active volcanoes on Earth. Not surprisingly, parts of Réunion Island are a sweeping and stark landscape of wide calderas and rocky plains. But there's another side to Réunion, and our image gives us a hint. Many areas are lush with moss-covered mountains, thick forests and hundreds of waterfalls. We're looking at Bassin de la Paix, a pool fed by the River des Roches as it cascades over a rugged cliff.
