The name Chapada Diamantina translates to 'an area of steep cliffs' and 'diamonds', and while the former is obvious from our image, the second half of the name involves a bit of history. This region of Bahia State in northeastern Brazil was once the destination of those looking to strike it rich with diamond mining. Today, this monumental landscape of high plateaus and wide valleys is mostly set aside as a protected eco-reserve: Chapada Diamantina National Park is truly a pristine jewel (excuse the pun) of Brazil, rich in semi-arid vegetation and home to much wildlife, including the hooded visorbearer, a particularly beautiful hummingbird.
