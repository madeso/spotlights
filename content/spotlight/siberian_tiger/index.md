Male Siberian tigers can weigh more than 400 pounds and measure over 10 feet from nose to tip of tail. In their natural habitat, the mountainous Sikhote-Alin region of the Russian Far East, Siberian tigers are endangered. Poaching is one of the primary problems, but habitat loss has also been a factor in their decline. These strikingly beautiful tigers can also be found in parts of China, and the Chinese and Russian governments have made joint efforts to strengthen regulations to protect the animals. A portion of Sikhote-Alin was placed on UNESCO's World Heritage List in 2001, in part to help preserve the habitat of the Siberian tiger.
