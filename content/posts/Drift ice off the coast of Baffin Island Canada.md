Our image features a waterline view of an ice floe in Admiralty Inlet off the west coast of Baffin Island. This is a super-sized landscape – and seascape – located in Canada's remote northeast. Baffin is the country's largest island, as well as being the world's fifth-largest. Just getting to this region of Nunavut, the country's newest designated territory, is an undertaking, as the whole area is in the remote Canadian Arctic. Officially established on 1 April 1999, Nunavut is a land of superlatives. It's the northernmost territory in Canada, the largest and boasts the smallest population. About that population: the indigenous people of the region have been settled here for 4,000 years.
