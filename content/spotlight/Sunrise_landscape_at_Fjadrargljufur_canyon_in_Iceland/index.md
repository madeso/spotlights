[Fjaðrárgljúfur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fja%C3%B0r%C3%A1rglj%C3%BAfur) is a canyon in south east Iceland. The Fjaðrá river flows through it. The canyon has steep walls and winding water. It is up to 100 m deep and about 2 kilometres (1.2 miles) long. It is located near the Ring Road, not far from the village of Kirkjubæjarklaustur.
