The Netherlands is already ahead of the curve when it comes to reducing car traffic and providing ample park and recreation space. Houten may just be the gold standard for Dutch city planning, though. As development of the Ultrecht province grew, special attention was paid to the layout of Houten, a small community about 30 miles from Amsterdam. City planners lent particular consideration to the arrangement of housing, public spaces and access to rail lines and bike lanes. This spot interested urban planners long ago, too: in the Old Town centre of Houten, the streets still follow the pattern laid out by the Romans when this area was occupied.
