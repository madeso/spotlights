Monumental rock formations make up the otherworldly landscape of this protected desert wilderness. The rocks here in Valley of Fire State Park are mostly red Aztec sandstone. Due to their colour, the formations can appear to be on fire when sunlight hits them just right, which inspired the park's name. Although most of Nevada is covered by the Great Basin Desert, the Mojave Desert stakes its claim here further south. And the red sandstone so common in the Valley of Fire is also abundant in another of southern Nevada's wilderness hot spots – Red Rock Canyon National Conservation Area.
