Castle Hill is a particularly apt name for this lighthouse situated on Narragansett Bay, since it appears as if it were wholly carved from the sea cliff like some mediaeval fortification. An icon of Newport since its completion in 1890, the conical lighthouse is made of granite blocks and iron. At 10 metres, it’s certainly not the tallest lighthouse in Rhode Island but it may well be the most photogenic.

Long popular as a backdrop for tourist snapshots and wedding photos, Castle Hill Lighthouse recently underwent something of a cosmetic overhaul. Those long streaks we see in our image are lines of rust coming from the metal railing and lens housing. In 2021, the lighthouse got a thorough power-washing and three coats of paint, beautifying a beloved landmark.
