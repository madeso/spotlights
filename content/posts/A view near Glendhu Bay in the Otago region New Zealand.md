It's a fair bet that most visitors to the 12,000-square-mile wonderland that is New Zealand's Otago region will find the place mesmerising. Scenery doesn't get much more dramatic than what's on offer here: rich farmland, lush forests, snow-capped mountains and wide river valleys stretch across this portion of the South Island . There are magnificent lakes here as well, including Lake Wānaka , whose shores are tucked below the mountains at Glendhu Bay in our image. It's easy to see why Otago is a hub for outdoor sports and recreation year-round. Here in the Southern Alps, anglers and water sport enthusiasts gather during the summer, while skiers and snowboarders hit the slopes in the winter.—
