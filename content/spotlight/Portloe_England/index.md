It seems perfectly fitting that Portloe should be set within an officially designated Cornwall Area of Outstanding Natural Beauty. And few would argue with this snug little village's inclusion in a Condé Nast Traveler roundup of some of the most beautiful spots in the country. Portloe is indeed a sweet scenic jewel of southwest England, and began—like many Cornish seaside towns—as a busy fishing port. Visitors here can stay over in a historic harborside hotel, or day-trip by doing a detour from the nearby South West Coast Path for lunch or a leisurely afternoon tea.
