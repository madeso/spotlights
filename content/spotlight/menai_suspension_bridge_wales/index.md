When the Menai Suspension Bridge was opened to traffic on January 30, 1826, it was the largest suspension road bridge in the world. Its 1,368 foot-length was needed to span the often wild and woolly Menai Strait between Anglesey and the mainland of Wales. Before the bridge was built, crossing back and forth to this largest of Welsh islands meant braving the whirlpools and strong tides of the strait. Beyond its undeniable practicality, the span stands as a testament to the talents of its designer, Thomas Telford, one of the greatest engineers of Great Britain's Industrial Revolution.
