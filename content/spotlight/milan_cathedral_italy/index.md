Milan Cathedral took more than 600 years to build, but what this dusky shot of the Piazza del Duomo in Italy's second-biggest city doesn't show is that the construction work never truly ends. The 21st century has been a time of major restoration for both the interior and—with the assistance of copious scaffolding—the exterior and spires of the Duomo, as it's fondly known by Milan residents. Much of the renovation work has been funded by donations, including a program that allowed locals and businesses (and even enamored tourists) to 'adopt' one of the cathedral's 135 spires.
