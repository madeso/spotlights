Fantastic photo ops abound on the Indonesian isle of Nusa Lembongan, but things really look spectacular when you get above it all. Our image features a seabird's-eye view of one of the island's most dramatic spots, a portion of its coastline known as Devil's Tears. Here, steep cliffs rise over the roiling surf, and the many coves and hollows at the base of the cliffs create massive geysers of sea water where the ocean meets the shore. Not far from the booming waves are large tidepools, home to a rich array of marine life. As epic as the Devil's Tears area is, it's just a small portion of the incredible scenery that graces the Nusa Islands.
