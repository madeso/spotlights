The Snæfellsnes Peninsula in western Iceland is a land of almost dreamlike proportions: sharp mountain peaks, roaring waterfalls, black rock coastlines and ice-choked fjords are just a few of the dramatic natural features of this extraordinary area. Not surprisingly, even the lighthouses here are eye-catching. Our image features Svörtuloft Lighthouse, a bright orange beacon perched on the westernmost part of Snæfellsnes Peninsula. Dating from the early 1930s, the lighthouse is a vivid photo op on its own, but anyone visiting this far-flung spot is sure to take myriad pictures of the surrounding ancient lava fields, dark sea cliffs and crashing waves.
