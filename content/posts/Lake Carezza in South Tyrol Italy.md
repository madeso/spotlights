The western Dolomites are an incredible sight all on their own – bare limestone massifs that rise so high they catch the clouds with their jagged peaks. But add a sparkling turquoise lake bordered by evergreen forests and you really have an unforgettable scene. Lake Carezza (aka Karersee) isn't a particularly large lake, and a walk around it takes only about a half an hour. But it's the lake's setting – and the extraordinary colour of its water – that make it a popular destination for those exploring this corner of the Trentino-Alto Adige. And after the sun sets and Lake Carezza is bathed in shadows, the place is a great spot for stargazing.
