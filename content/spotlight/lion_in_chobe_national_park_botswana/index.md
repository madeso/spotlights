When it comes to adult male lions, the general rule is: The darker and fuller the mane, the healthier and more dominant the lion. This applies primarily to African lions in the wild, and doesn't hold for lions in captivity. In some zoos, captive lions may grow even heavier manes regardless of their health status. This is largely due to cooler ambient temperatures, which promote thicker hair growth for insulation. (The big fella in our image is resting in the warm grasslands of Chobe National Park in Botswana.) Lions once roamed across much of Africa, Europe, and Asia, but their distribution has significantly diminished. In 1996, they were officially listed as Vulnerable on the International Union for Conservation of Nature (IUCN) Red List of Threatened Species
