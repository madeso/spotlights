Walkway and pavilion in Khao Sam Roi Yot National Park, Thailand
The first coastal national park established in Thailand, Khao Sam Roi Yot stretches nearly 40 square miles across the hills and wetlands inland from the Gulf of Thailand. Our image features a view of Bueng Bau Nature Observation Center and its swath of freshwater marsh, part of the country's largest. The fauna living in Khao Sam Roi Yot are just as incredible as the flora. One of the park's most elusive creatures is the fishing cat. About the size of a domestic house cat, these muscular felines are, naturally, found near water. Unlike the average house cat, the fishing cat will eagerly dive into water and even swim, all in pursuit of a meal.
