Located in the far northwestern reaches of the country, County Donegal claims the longest mainland coastline in Ireland. Parts of the shore are deeply indented with sea loughs (or lochs), and Fanad Lighthouse is planted atop a headland bounded by Lough Swilly on one side and Mulroy Bay on the other. A renowned scenic route, the Wild Atlantic Way, passes through this area. Winding through more than 1,500 miles of spectacular scenery, it's one of the world's longest defined coastal routes. It stretches from Malin Head—roughly 40 miles north of the area in our image—south to the Old Head of Kinsale on the Celtic Sea coast.
