Rio Grande do Sul and Santa Catarina are Brazilian states located in the southernmost part of the country. Serra Geral National Park is in two sections, with a portion in each state, and with Aparados da Serra National Park and its spectacular Itaimbezinho Canyon sandwiched in between. In this region, the landscape ranges from high plateaus to sheer cliffs to canyon floors, and much of it is within the lush Atlantic Forest biome. Our image features a magisterial view across Fortaleza Canyon. All-terrain vehicles or sturdy hiking boots are advised if you want to get to this vantage point, but it looks to be well worth the journey.
