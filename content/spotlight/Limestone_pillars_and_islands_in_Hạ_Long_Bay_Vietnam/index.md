There are certainly modern touches to the villages and cities along Hạ Long (Descending Dragon) Bay. But thanks to the unusual landscape, plenty of scenes along this jagged coast retain the charm of centuries ago. Local legend holds that a mountain dragon created Hạ Long Bay as it thrashed around on its descent from a nearby peak, breaking up the coastline with its tail and eventually tumbling into the sea, creating the bay. (That’s more compelling than the geology lesson we had planned.)

In the tropical forests atop many of the karst islands are the lizards, birds, and monkeys typical of this part of Vietnam. And then there are the native breeds of semi-feral bantams that peck out a living on the craggy outcrops.
