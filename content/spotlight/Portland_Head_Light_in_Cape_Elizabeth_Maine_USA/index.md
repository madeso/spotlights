The oldest lighthouse in Maine also happens to be one of the most picturesque in the United States. The setting is undeniably spectacular: a rambling, rocky headland reaching into Portland Harbour at Cape Elizabeth. The lighthouse tower was built in 1791, on the request of no less than the country's first president, George Washington. A century later, a large keeper's house was added and Portland Head Light remained manned until it was fully automated in 1989. Although no keepers remain, their red-roofed home is now a maritime museum dedicated to preserving the unique history of this important, and superbly scenic, lighthouse.
