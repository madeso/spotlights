Vulpes vulpes is the scientific name of the red fox, a species of fox that is found throughout Europe, Asia, North America, and North Africa. It is the largest of the true foxes and has a wide natural distribution. It has also been introduced to Australia, where it is considered an invasive species. There are several subspecies of the red fox, such as the American red fox (Vulpes vulpes fulva).
