Lake Hibara takes on a quiet serenity during the colder months, when snow blankets the lake's islets and fog hangs low over the water. But this lake is beautiful any time of the year, owing to its setting amid the green forested hills and high mountain peaks of central Japan. Looking at our peaceful image, it may be hard to believe that this lake was created in a cataclysm: on 15 July 1888, the stratovolcano Mount Bandai erupted in a fury, altering the geography of the area in massive ways, changing the courses of rivers and creating a number of new lakes, including Hibara. The lake lies within the third-largest national park in Japan – Bandai-Asahi National Park.
