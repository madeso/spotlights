Standing 118 feet above street level, Henderson Waves is the highest pedestrian bridge in Singapore—and it certainly makes quite the splash. The nearly 900-foot-long span is a standout in the Southern Ridges, an area of lush, interconnected parks. Opened in 2008, the bridge features seven sculptural steel arcs that ripple along its span. These sweeping forms rise and fall like waves, giving the bridge its name and creating a visual rhythm. When the weather gets a little too sunny (or too rainy—this is Singapore, after all), sightseers can retreat under built-in alcoves. And at night? That's when the lights come on, transforming the bridge into a glowing architectural showpiece.
