The Apennine Mountains cross through the Umbrian region of central Italy, and there, in the high elevation of Monti Sibillini National Park, is a tiny village called Castelluccio. The farming community dates back at least to the 13th century, and attracts skiers and hikers. The town is famous for a gourmet legume: Castelluccio lentils are considered an epicurean treat and can be a little more expensive than the typical bag you buy at the grocery store. And when these fields of lentils are in bloom, the plains of Castelluccio transform into a watercolor wonderland, rivaling the region's colorful sunsets, like the pink-toned beauty in our picture.
