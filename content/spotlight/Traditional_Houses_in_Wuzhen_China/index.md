Located in Zhejiang province on China's east coast, Wuzhen is a both a living, breathing town and a time capsule of the region's culture and history. One of the Yangtze River's water towns, much of Wuzhen is built above canals, and it's divided into six districts, all of which feature different aspects of the town's traditions. Wooden buildings bordered by cobblestone streets, ancient bridges and rows of traditional homes – like those in our image – greet visitors. Some neighbourhoods in Wuzhen feature historical reenactments and craftspeople working at centuries-old trades. Wuzhen workshops still turn out exquisite crafts, such as intricate wood carvings and silk textiles.
