Balloon safaris are popular in Kenya's Maasai Mara National Reserve, and it's not hard to see why. From this high vantage point, a passenger may see some of the elephants, lions, leopards and cheetahs that inhabit this treasured, 600-square-mile reserve. And they may also get a glimpse of a neighbouring park in another country: 'The Mara' adjoins Serengeti National Park just over the border in Tanzania, and together the Mara-Serengeti park system protects some 9,700 square miles.The greatest show a high-flying visitor here could witness typically begins in May, when the rainy season in the southern Serengeti ends. Herds of zebras numbering in the hundreds of thousands begin to migrate north across the plains from Tanzania toward Kenya's Maasai Mara region. Joining the zebras will be 1.5 million blue wildebeest that follow the same migratory route. By July, the herds will arrive in Maasai Mara, only to leave in November and return to Tanzania.
