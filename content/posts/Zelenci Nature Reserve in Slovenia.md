Not far from the ski resort of Kranjska Gora is a stunning Alpine wilderness preserve. The scenic wonders of this place are myriad—the soaring peaks of the Julian Alps rise above thick stands of forest, sweeping meadows, and the turquoise-green Lake Zelenci, featured here in our image. It’s said the name of the lake, and the entire park, was inspired by the brilliant waters, as 'zelen' is the Slovenian word for green.

The lake is fed by a spring where the mostly underground course of Nadiža Creek reemerges. As breathtaking as it is, Zelenci is just a taste of what lies a few miles south of here: Slovenia’s only national park, the vast Triglav National Park.
