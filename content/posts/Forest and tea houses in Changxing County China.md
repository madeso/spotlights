Lush bamboo forests surround Datang Gong Tribute Tea Park, tucked within the foothills of Changxing. This county in eastern China, part of the wider Huzhou city area, was established during the Jin Empire more than 1,700 years ago. Needless to say, Changxing has an extensive cultural heritage, including in the realm of – you guessed it – tea. Datang Gong Tea House was said to be one of the first tea-processing plants in China, and visitors to the site are treated to collections of historic objects and re-creations of tea-making machinery, as well as some warm pots of the reviving brew itself.
