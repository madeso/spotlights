Although it's a very small island—just over half a square mile in area—it sure packs in the culture and charm. Manabeshima sits among other islands and islets in the Seto Inland Sea of southwestern Japan, and it stands out from its neighbors in a few ways. The main hubs are two quiet fishing villages, where most of the roughly 200 residents live. A healthy collection of free-roaming felines also populates the place, making it one of Japan's renowned 'cat islands.' Manabeshima has also lately become something of an international destination, with unexpected fame stemming from French author Florent Chavouet's illustrated book detailing the island's idyllic vibe.
