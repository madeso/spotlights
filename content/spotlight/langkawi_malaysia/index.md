Over the past few decades, Langkawi has become a favorite destination for beach-loving tourists of all nationalities, and taking a look around the island in northwestern Malaysia gives us a clue why. Besides many long stretches of white-sand coastline backed by verdant rainforests, the main island also features mountain peaks and peaceful bays—we're seeing a tableau of both in our image of Telga Harbour and Perdana Quay Lighthouse. Langkawi is the name of both the main island, and the larger archipelago all around it, consisting of roughly 99 islands and islets in the Strait of Malacca.
