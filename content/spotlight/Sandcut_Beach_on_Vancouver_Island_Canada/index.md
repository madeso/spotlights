At 290 miles long and 62 miles wide, Vancouver Island, part of the province of British Columbia in Canada, holds the record for the biggest island on the west coast of all of North or South America. And while many of the natural vistas here are epic, sometimes it's worth it to zoom in on one special spot. Our image of a coastal waterfall was taken on Sandcut Beach, one of a series of beautiful beaches on Vancouver Island's southern shore. Many of these beaches can only be reached after long forest hikes – but for many people who visit, these nature walks are as good as the pounding surf that awaits at the end of the trail.
