The Salzkammergut, an area of awe-inspiring limestone mountains, lush alpine forests and massive glacial lakes, is one of Austria's most popular resort areas. And while many people who visit enjoy a quiet paddle on a calm lake, some like to get beneath it all. We're looking at a reason why: the crystal-clear waters of Hinterer Langbathsee, one of a duo of lakes (along with Vorderer) known as the Langbathseen. And while they're not the largest lakes in the region, they do offer some of the most transparent water around. These lakes are oligotrophic, meaning they have little to no aquatic vegetation or algae, so the visibility is excellent.
