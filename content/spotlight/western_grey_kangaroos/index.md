Lucky Bay, in Western Australia's Cape Le Grand National Park, attracts many visitors to its pristine beach, including a colony of friendly kangaroos who seem to enjoy soaking up the sun as much as the rest of us. Arguably the most recognizable wildlife symbol of Australia (some koalas may disagree), kangaroos are the only large animals that use hopping as a means to get around. Western grey kangaroos, like the pair in our image, can reach 4 feet tall and weigh 120 pounds, making them some of the world's largest macropods. Who takes the kangaroo crown for height? That would be the red kangaroo, which can exceed 6 feet tall.
