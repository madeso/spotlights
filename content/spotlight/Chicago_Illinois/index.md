Our image was taken over Navy Pier, and the waters of Lake Michigan are frozen over, looking like icy paving stones in the foreground of the city's skyscrapers. Want to experience this sweeping view in person? Bring your warmest coat—Chicago winters are notoriously frigid, with an average daily temperature of roughly 36 degrees Fahrenheit, and a bone-chilling record low of 27 below zero set in 1985. During a particularly brutal cold snap in 2014, a meteorologist coined the term 'Chiberia'—a combo of Chicago and Siberia—to convey the depth of the chill.
