Palawan is a Philippine province, and it's here you'll find El Nido, the municipality seen in our image. The word 'municipality' seems far too mundane a label for this breathtaking landscape of limestone cliffs, coral reefs, white-sand beaches and startlingly clear water. Palawan may be the country's least populated area, but its spectacular scenery attracts tourists from all over the world, mostly to the many islands and their coastlines. During certain parts of the year, these tranquil waters are certainly buzzing. Hardier visitors may want to venture further inland, where there are fewer people and many rugged trails to help explore the wilderness.
