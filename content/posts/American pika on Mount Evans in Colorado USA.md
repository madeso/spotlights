There's no denying the cute factor when discussing pikas, as the pint-sized mammals are the very definition of adorable. But these small relatives of rabbits are also some of the hardiest creatures on Earth – many live at very high elevations, in the alpine zone above the tree line, where food can be scarce and the weather harsh. The animal seen here, in all its fuzzy glory, is an American pika living on the rocky slopes of Mount Evans in Colorado.

In recent years the American pika has become a kind of early warning sign for climate change. Here in Colorado, these animals are superbly adapted to their cool alpine environment, but will perish in warmer temperatures. Monitoring their populations will alert researchers to even slight changes in alpine temperatures.
