Located in southern British Columbia, Canada's Okanagan region is a land of many layers. Wilderness abounds, as our aerial image illustrates: this verdant scene is a part of the wetlands and forest near the shores of Hydraulic Lake. Besides idyllic places to hike and camp, the region is full of fruit orchards. In fact, fruit crops, such as pears and apricots, were once the main industry here. But in recent years, the Okanagan has become a popular holiday spot, and some farms have been converted to resorts. Other orchards have replaced their fruit trees with grapes to supply the burgeoning wine industry.
