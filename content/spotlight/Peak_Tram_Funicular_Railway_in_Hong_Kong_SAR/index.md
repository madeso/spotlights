Peak Tram funicular railway in Hong Kong SAR
At 511 metres in elevation, Victoria Peak is Hong Kong Island's highest hill. Viewpoints near the summit offer spectacular views of Victoria Harbour and the thickets of skyscrapers lining its shores. How best to get to the top of 'The Peak', as it's colloquially known? Perhaps via the cheery little funicular rail cars that climb steeply on their tracks for a five-minute ride of just under a mile. Riders are let off at a terminal inside Peak Tower, a gleaming glass shopping complex and viewing platform roughly 150 metres below the actual summit of Victoria Peak.

The Peak Tram has been making its way up and down its sheer hillside route since 1888, and is thought to be the first funicular railway ever built in Asia. Hugely popular with tourists, the entire system received a complete overhaul and reopened in 2022.
