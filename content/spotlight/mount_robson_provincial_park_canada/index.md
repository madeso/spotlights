Without a doubt, Mount Robson is a peak worthy of a provincial park. Standing tall at nearly 13,000 feet in elevation, not only is it the highest point in the Canadian Rockies, but it's also an astoundingly scenic massif in a land full of breathtaking landscapes. What else does this pristine park offer? More than 800 square miles of British Columbia wilderness, including three massive lakes (Berg, Kinney, and Yellowhead), Canada's second-deepest cave, and an array of endangered wildlife, like grizzly bears, mountain caribou, and wolverines. Bonus: The park is only about an hour's drive from the equally impressive, but more touristed, Jasper National Park.
