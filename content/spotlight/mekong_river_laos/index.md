One of the great rivers of the world, the Mekong flows for roughly 2,700 miles from its source in the Tibetan Plateau to its outlet on the South China Sea in Vietnam. Here we see a portion of the river coursing through Laos, not far from Luang Prabang. A transboundary river passing through six countries, the Mekong flows in the west of Laos, forming the border with Thailand for much of its journey. This waterway is a lifegiving resource, as well as a sheer spectacle of natural beauty, and tour boats are often seen plying the Mekong in this scenery-rich part of Laos.
