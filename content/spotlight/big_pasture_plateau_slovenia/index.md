A thick layer of snow blankets the hills and cloaks the evergreen trees of northern Slovenia's Big Pasture Plateau. Here, rocky karst terrain gives way to lush alpine pastureland, all of it dotted by spruce and pine trees. This area of the Kamnik–Savinja Alps has been a place where people gather since Neolithic times, and was used for seasonal cattle grazing throughout the medieval period. Faithful reconstructions of herdsman's huts cluster in the Velika Planina settlement on the plateau, and are a popular attraction, especially in the verdant spring and summer. What's to do in winter? The answer is obvious: lots and lots of skiing.
