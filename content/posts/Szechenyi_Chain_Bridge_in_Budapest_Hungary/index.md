Budapest, the sprawling capital of Hungary, lies across more than 200 square miles. The western section of the city is known as Buda, and the eastern is called (you guessed it) Pest. Our image features the Széchenyi Chain Bridge, a major link between the two parts. The span across the Danube opened just after the Hungarian Revolution in 1849 but was nearly demolished by German troops during World War II. The bridge's two massive stone towers were not destroyed during the Siege of Budapest and Széchenyi Chain Bridge was completely rebuilt and reopened in 1949, once again connecting the two halves of the resilient city.
