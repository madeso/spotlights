Mountains and lakes are Carinthia's best-known natural features, and while the lakes here are certainly splendid, our image features part of the incredible mountain range that marches through this, the southernmost Austrian state. The Carnic Alps are a subrange of the Southern Limestone Alps and form a roughly 62-mile border between Austria and Italy.


Carinthia's climate can be harsh—hot and humid summers followed by long, cold winters. But there are compensations, and our image shows a major one: The truly breathtaking spectacle of rugged peaks rising skyward above verdant valleys. The state's abundance of medieval villages nestled into the crags of the foothills, and its larger historic towns spread across valleys, add to the staggering scenery. And don't forget those lakes—there are reportedly more than 1,200 of them within Carinthia's borders.
