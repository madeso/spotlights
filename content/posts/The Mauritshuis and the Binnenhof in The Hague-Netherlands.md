The Hague, located about an hour south of Amsterdam , is known for being the center of global law—the city hosts both the International Court of Justice and the International Criminal Court . But this historic city is also home to many sites of national importance, including the buildings we see in our image. Here on the shores of Hofvijver lake sit structures dedicated to culture and governing. On the right is the long expanse of the Binnenhof , originally a 13th-century Gothic castle that's been the seat of the Dutch government for more than 400 years. The building on the left houses one of the world's greatest collections of Dutch art. The Mauritshuis museum's cultural treasures include hundreds of works from the Dutch Golden Age , including paintings by Rembrandt, Hals, Holbein—and perhaps the most renowned canvas of all—Vermeer's Girl with a Pearl Earring .
