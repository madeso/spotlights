Part sun-soaked southern European playground, part protected eco-park, the island of Alonnisos is a gem in the Sporades island chain. Our image was taken from the bright blue Grecian skies, looking across the aquamarine Aegean toward Agios Dimitrios beach. The island is popular with visitors just looking to relax, but it's also an important refuge for nature: It's part of the Alonnisos Marine Park, Europe's largest protected marine area. Here, coniferous forests on the land, and Posidonia oceanica beds on the seafloor, are kept pristine. Wildlife enjoying the high life include monk seals, bottlenose dolphins, and beaked whales.
