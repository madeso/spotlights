A superdense neutron star left behind by a stellar explosion is spewing out a blizzard of extremely high-energy particles into the expanding debris field known as the Crab Nebula. To capture the visual cacophony of this chaos, 25 individual images taken by the Hubble Space Telescope were assembled, resulting in our spectacular composite image. This outerspace wonder gave a brilliant show when it first appeared in the sky: The Crab Nebula is the remnant of a supernova explosion recorded by Chinese astronomers in 1054, bright enough to be seen in daylight for weeks and at night for nearly two years.
