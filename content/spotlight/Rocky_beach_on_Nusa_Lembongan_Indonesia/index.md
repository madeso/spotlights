The Nusa Islands are a trio of islands lying in the Indian Ocean southeast of Bali. The rocky shore and powder blue water in our image is found on Nusa Lembongan. Neither the largest isle (Nusa Penida) nor the smallest (Nusa Ceningan), Lembongan is the most popular with tourists, and not simply because of its stunning good looks – the other islands in the group are just as photogenic. What sets this island apart are the hotels, eateries and activities catering to visitors. That doesn’t mean that Lembongan is a bustling tourist hub like parts of Bali can be; there's still quite the laid-back vibe on Lembongan.

But it's not all rest and relaxation here: two of the most buzzed-about activities on Nusa Lembongan are its world-class surfing and extraordinary scuba diving. That blue water provides thrills from top to bottom.
