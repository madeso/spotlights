Nearly all the land in Amsterdam was reclaimed from natural canals and rivers, and the waters of the North Sea. A complex system of sea walls, dykes and other engineering feats keeps the city dry. As a result, most of Amsterdam is at least 1.8 metres below sea level, and some locations are even lower. Roughly 1,200 bridges cross more than 60 miles of canals in this capital of the Netherlands, and while residents do get around by boat and barge, it sometimes seems like the entire city of 850,000 is on two wheels. It's been estimated that Amsterdam households boast an average of 1.91 bicycles each.
