Our twilight view of the sparkling Auckland skyline was taken from Mount Eden, a dormant volcano. While not the capital of New Zealand (that's Wellington), Auckland is the country's largest city, with an urban population of roughly a million and a half. A panoramic shot of Auckland taken from atop a volcano isn't unusual, as much of the city is built over a dormant, yet not entirely extinct, basaltic volcanic field. Over 50 volcanoes dot the city's surrounding area, but modern eruptions in the region are rare, the last occurring in the 15th century.

The Auckland volcanic field benefited the area's Indigenous people: the Māori built forts with strategic vantage points atop various volcanic cones, and the rich volcanic soil proved excellent for raising crops, including the kumara, a kind of sweet potato.
