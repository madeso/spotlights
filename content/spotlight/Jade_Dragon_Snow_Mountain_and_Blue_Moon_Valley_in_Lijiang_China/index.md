Here in the Yunnan province of southwest China, the snow-capped massif known as Jade Dragon Snow Mountain (Mount Yulongxue) dominates the landscape, and in its foothills in the Blue Moon Valley, lakes and rivers take on an exquisite jade hue. (Fast fact: copper ions in the water impart that bright colour.) Close to this stunning natural scene is a historic treasure: the Old Town of Lijiang. The Tea Horse Road, an ancient trade route, once passed through this canal-side village of stone and wooden bridges. The ancient settlement has been called the 'Venice of Asia', and the entire town was inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1997.
