In an effort to reclaim waterfront land for open space, city officials in Singapore commissioned this park in 2006. Attractions include the Flower Dome and Cloud Forest conservatories seen in the left of our image, and the Supertree Grove in the center right. Exotic ferns, vines, orchids, bromeliads, and other plants trail up the 'trunks' of the Supertrees, which can be as high as 164 feet. Surrounded by vertical gardens and tropical plants in this 250-acre park, it's easy to forget that you're next to a bustling downtown. The grove is astounding enough during the day, but at night the soaring structures and an elevated walkway shine and shimmer in a spectacular light show.
