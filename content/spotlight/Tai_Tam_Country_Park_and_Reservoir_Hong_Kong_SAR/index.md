Providing an adequate supply of fresh water to a city of over 7 million people is no easy task. Hong Kong SAR's many reservoirs help keep up with the demand, and these massive engineering projects are not only practical but often picturesque as well. Tai Tam Country Park, in the eastern corner of Hong Kong Island, is built around a group of four reservoirs that sit amidst some of the most beautiful – and quiet – scenery anywhere in the bustling metropolis.

Tall mountains, including the second-highest peak on Hong Kong Island – Mount Parker – rise up around the lakes. Hiking paths snake their way around the slopes and peaks. These trails offer visitors a panorama of the park's glimmering lakes and lush greenery, as well as spectacular views of the city's forest of skyscrapers in Kowloon.
