Washed by the Tasman Sea, the beach at Hamilton's Gap is far flung and not easy to get to—even its closest city, Auckland, is a roughly two-hour drive away. The journey, though, is worth it: Sweeping black sand beaches meet rugged coastal ranges, and in between are the highest sand dunes in the Auckland region. The beach is as peaceful as it is breathtaking, which is something you could say about pretty much the entire Āwhitu Peninsula. This sparsely populated area boasts a lengthy shoreline, plenty of wooded inland areas, and rivers large and small, one of which cuts through the landscape to create Hamilton's Gap.
