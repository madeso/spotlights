Stretching 55 miles in length and nearly 30 miles across at its widest point, Lake Kivu is one of the seven major African Great Lakes. A border lake, Kivu's western shores are located in the Democratic Republic of the Congo, while its eastern shores lie in Rwanda, where our image was taken. Sheer size and an international border aren't the only remarkable things about Lake Kivu—it's one of the few lakes in the world that experience limnic eruptions. These outflows of carbon dioxide can be life threatening, and are estimated to occur roughly once every thousand years.
