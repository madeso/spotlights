On the east coast of Trinidad is what many people consider one of the island's most beautiful stretches. Manzanilla Beach lacks the heavy tourist infrastructure of other spots in the country, so it attracts fewer partygoers, and more peace-seekers. A large Ramsar wetland borders the beach inland, while Atlantic waves meet the coast. That part of the Manzanilla experience is a bit less peaceful: Frequently windy conditions and strong currents mean it's not really suitable for swimming. And yet, some visitors actually swim to get here—Leatherback sea turtles arrive on the beach starting in March, and hatchlings appear through the summer months.
