The Landmannalaugar region in the highlands of southern Iceland is not easy to get to, but it rewards those who brave the trek in. Visitors reach the area on horseback or 4-wheel-drive vehicles, or by hiking along the Laugavegur trail. Nearby volcanoes created those prominent stripes in the mountains. It's called flow banding, and it results in rock formations of banded rhyolite. There's even more local color during the summer months, when travelers to Landmannalaugar may come across a green school bus known as Fjallafang – a coffee shop on wheels that caters to hikers.
