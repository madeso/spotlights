Stretching across two counties in the heart of South Korea, Woraksan National Park is a hiker's paradise. Trails of all challenge levels abound, from short and sweet walks on the shores of Chungju Lake to steep treks that really test your endurance—the Worak Mountain Villa trail will net you 3,543 feet of total ascent, the biggest elevation gain in the park. Our image gives a preview of what you'll see after finishing the Jebibong Peak hike, a challenging 2.6 mile-route that takes about 2.5 hours to complete. Some of the park's routes have hikers passing by sublime temples nestled at forest edge, reminding visitors of the region's rich cultural heritage
