Two countries share the spectacular nature show we see in our image. In Angola these multi-tiered waterfalls are known as Quedas do Monte Negro, and here in Namibia – where our photo was taken – the cascades are called Epupa Falls. The river that feeds these enormous cascades is the Cunene (aka Kunene), originating in the Angolan highlands. After reaching Namibia, the river tumbles into steep chasms, creating a most incredible national – and natural – border. In this rather arid landscape dotted with fig and baobab trees, the massive cataract of water makes a powerful display. The spectacle brings visitors who make the trip here across rough terrain in four-wheel-drive vehicles.
