The iconic view from Zabriskie Point is one of the reasons why photographers and tourists flock to this spot all year round. Weather and water carved out these badlands for eons, resulting in stunning striped rock formations with an array of colors. The darker material on several peaks was formed by lava from an ancient volcanic eruption, making some of these ridges higher than others. Nestled in Death Valley National Park, California, Zabriskie Point is composed of sediments such as mudstone that was found in Furnace Creek Lake, which dried up 5 million years ago. The park also features snow-frosted peaks, lush wildflower meadows, and small oases that provide a reprieve from the endless desert sun.
