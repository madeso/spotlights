Hong Kong SAR has more than a dozen districts, and we're looking at a drone's-eye view of perhaps the greenest and most relaxed. And while that's quite a claim for an area of roughly 300,000 people and many clusters of high-rise buildings, it's all about the context. In a sprawling city of more than 7 million people known for its thickets of skyscrapers and bustling financial centre, the northern Tai Po District has one of the city's lowest population densities, as well as its largest woodland area (the Tai Po Kau Nature Reserve).

The Tai Po District also boasts forested mountains, farming valleys, small villages, a smattering of islands and a lush coastline – a slice of which is glimpsed in our image.
