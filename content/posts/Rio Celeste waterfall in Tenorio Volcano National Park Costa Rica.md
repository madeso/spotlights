The jewel of Guanacaste Province in northern Costa Rica is the rainforest wilderness called Tenorio Volcano National Park. And while the stratovolcano it's named after is impressive, most visitors are just as thrilled by what we're seeing in our image: the Rio Celeste river and waterfall. What causes the water's lovely powder-blue hue? That's thanks to a combination of calcium carbonate and high water acidity due to the nearby volcano. Geothermal activity is also responsible for the park's hot springs and small geysers. Tenorio Volcano is inactive, and trails lead to the summit for superb views over the verdant park.
