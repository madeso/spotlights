On a clear day from the summit of Cerro La Campana, the highest point in La Campana National Park, you can see both the glimmering Pacific Ocean and a towering line of the Andes Mountains. The scenic highlights don't end there. A decidedly less strenuous hike will still take you through the Mediterranean-like ecosystem of the park: Rare Chilean wine palms, Quillay trees, and hedgehog cacti decorate the scrubland and arid hills. Endemic wildlife thrives here, and visitors might see some of the same types of creatures Charles Darwin observed when he visited the region in 1834, including vizcachas, Chilean mockingbirds, and elusive, nocturnal culpeo foxes
