The hike from the Grindelwald First cable car station to this mirror-like lake high in the Bernese Alps is known for being as gentle as it is scenic. During most of the roughly hour-long walk, visitors are treated to expansive views of rolling alpine meadows and craggy, imperial peaks. Some of the biggest names in the Alps loom above the lake: The Schreckhorn, Wetterhorn—and that tallest of all the Bernese Alps and most prominent peak in Switzerland—the Finsteraarhorn. They all line up for one of the best photo ops in the country.
