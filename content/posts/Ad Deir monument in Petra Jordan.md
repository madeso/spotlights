Like nearly every structure in the ancient city of Petra, Ad Deir was carved directly into the sandstone cliffs that line the valley. The monument boasts one of the most ornate, detailed façades of all the buildings here. Our image was taken from the roof of the structure, looking out toward a huge decorative urn that crowns the building. Ad Deir is considered an extraordinary example of Nabataean architecture, an ancient style that combines Mesopotamian, Phoenician, and Greek elements. Petra, in southern Jordan, is an invaluable historic site containing hundreds of intricately carved temples, tombs, and other archeological wonders, including arguably the most renowned building in this ancient city, Al-Khazneh, or The Treasury.
