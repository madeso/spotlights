Nicknamed ‘Thousand Islands’, this archipelago on the St Lawrence River at the border of the United States and Canada overdelivers: There are actually 1,864 official islands here. Ranging from more than 40 square miles to much smaller rock outcrops, the islands' sizes vary considerably. Strict rules govern what can be considered an island: it needs an area of at least one square foot of land that stays above water level all year, and a minimum of two living trees must be growing on it. The Thousand Islands are popular not only with visitors, but also homeowners, some of whom have built atop a small isle with only enough room for their dream cottage – and those two important trees.
