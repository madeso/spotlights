Rising to more than 12,000 feet in elevation, Mount Fuji is Japan's highest peak and foremost mountain icon. Fuji towers over the hills around Lake Ashi in our image, and is an active stratovolcano—although it last erupted in 1707, and the chance of another blast is said to be low. Artists have captured Fuji's magisterial form and iconic snow-capped peak for millennia. It's also a magnet for mountaineers, and much easier to summit than either Everest or Kilimanjaro. The arrival of first light on Fuji is called goraikō, and climbers start their trek in the wee hours so they can catch the sunrise at the mountain's peak.
