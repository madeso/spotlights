On the southern flank of the Mendip Hills is a limestone landscape of superlatives: Cheddar Gorge is considered England's deepest natural canyon, as well as where Britain's oldest complete human skeleton was found – the roughly 9,000-year-old Cheddar Man. And while the towering cliffs here are undeniably spectacular – some rise nearly 125 metres above the winding road – there's just as much wonder underground. A subterranean river has carved out a complex of caves full of stalagmites, stalactites and colonies of horseshoe bats. Some archaeologists believe that prehistoric people may have used the caves for ageing cheese, but they don't know if it tasted like, well, cheddar.
