If nearby Venice is not quite colorful enough for you, make your way to Burano. Though sometimes called an island, Burano is actually a tightly packed collection of small islands in the Venetian Lagoon of northern Italy. The main island is connected to three smaller isles by bridges. Legend holds that all the houses were painted in varying, bright tints so that fishermen could pick out their homes even while out casting nets. Today, if you want to paint your villa in Burano, you send a request to the local government, which will reply with the color options available to you. Looks to us like there's a pretty big palette.
